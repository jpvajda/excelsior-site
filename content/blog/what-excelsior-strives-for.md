Excelsior LLC is a Pain Reprocessing Therapy and somatic coaching practice. Based in Boulder, Colorado. Coaching by video anywhere.

## What we are working toward

Chronic pain is real. The brain can keep pain going even after the body has healed. Excelsior strives to help people understand that role, practice calmer attention to sensations, and move toward what feels good — alongside their medical care.

Coaching is not medical care. It does not replace a doctor, medication, or physical therapy.

## How coaching fits

The work follows a clear process: learn the brain's role, find your own evidence, befriend the pain, face what is underneath, and move toward what feels good. As the brain relearns safety, the alarm can quiet down and pain signals can turn down.
