Excelsior means "ever upward." That is the mission: help people living with chronic pain get to a higher place in their own lives and feel better.

Pain is real, and the brain can keep it going even after the body has healed. Through Pain Reprocessing Therapy and somatic coaching, you learn to meet pain with calm curiosity instead of fear, so the alarm can quiet down. Coaching is not medical care and does not replace your doctor.

## Where to start

Based in Boulder, Colorado. Coaching by video anywhere. [Book a free 30 minute discovery call](https://scheduler.zoom.us/excelsior-llc/prt-discovery-call) to see if it is a fit.
